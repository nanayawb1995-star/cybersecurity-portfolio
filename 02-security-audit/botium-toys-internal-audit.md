# Internal Security Audit – Botium Toys

## Framework Used
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

## Audit Scope
This audit evaluates Botium Toys’ internal IT security posture, regulatory compliance risks, and control effectiveness as the company expands its online and international presence.

---

## Administrative Controls

| Control | Implemented? |
|----------|--------------|
| Information Security Policy | No |
| Acceptable Use Policy | No |
| Incident Response Plan | No |
| Risk Assessment Process | Yes |
| Access Control Policy | No |

---

## Technical Controls

| Control | Implemented? |
|----------|--------------|
| Firewall Protection | Yes |
| Endpoint Protection | Yes |
| Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) | No |
| Encryption for Payment Data | No |
| Secure Password Policy | No |

---

## Physical Controls

| Control | Implemented? |
|----------|--------------|
| Secured Server Room | No |
| Surveillance Systems | No |
| Restricted Physical Access | No |

---

## Compliance Review

### PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry)
Compliant: No  
Risk: Potential fines, legal penalties, and reputational damage.

### GDPR (European Union Data Protection)
Compliant: No  
Risk: Regulatory fines up to 4% of annual global revenue.

---

## Overall Risk Assessment

Botium Toys faces high security and regulatory risk due to missing policies, lack of documented controls, absence of MFA, unclear encryption standards, and non-compliance with GDPR and PCI-DSS requirements.

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions
- Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Establish formal Information Security Policy
- Develop Incident Response Plan
- Begin PCI-DSS compliance review
- Encrypt customer payment data

### Short-Term Improvements
- Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Conduct GDPR gap analysis
- Deploy centralized logging and monitoring
- Conduct employee security awareness training

---

This audit demonstrates the application of NIST CSF principles, enterprise risk management analysis, and regulatory compliance evaluation.
